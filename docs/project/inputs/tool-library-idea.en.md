In a neighbourhood, many tools sleep in garages while other neighbours rent or buy the same
ones. I want a simple place where someone lists the tool they are willing to lend, where a
neighbour sees what is available nearby, and where the two agree on a date for the loan and
for the return. No money, no delivery: just trust between neighbours, and a clear history of
who has what.

*(Translated from the maintainer's French original, kept outside the repository: the project's
language is English.)*
